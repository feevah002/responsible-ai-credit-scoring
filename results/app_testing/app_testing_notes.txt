
APP TESTING NOTES

These test cases were selected from the real test split of the prepared Home Credit dataset.

Purpose:
- To test the app using real dataset examples rather than only imaginary manual inputs.
- To compare the app prediction with the actual TARGET value.
- To demonstrate correct predictions, errors, and affordability-risk cases.

Important:
- Actual TARGET = 0 means no repayment difficulty.
- Actual TARGET = 1 means repayment difficulty.
- Model prediction = 0 means predicted no repayment difficulty.
- Model prediction = 1 means predicted repayment difficulty.
- Risk probability is the predicted probability of repayment difficulty.
- The app remains a simplified prototype interface, while the full model evaluation uses all 62 trained features.
